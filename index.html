University design prototype · MEDS3888 Group 13

Helping young people find a therapist who fits, before their first session.

Thera-Fits is a website prototype for 18–24-year-olds who haven't started therapy yet. It asks what matters to them in plain language, then suggests three therapists who line up, and says where each one doesn't.

See the two versions

Why we built it

Starting is the hard part

We set out to understand why some young people never start therapy. As one person we interviewed put it, help is easy to find online; what's often missing is the trust to open up to someone, and knowing you'll find someone you're comfortable with.

Fit means more than a job title

Our interviews and survey pointed past qualifications: a therapist's background and culture, how they communicate, and whether they'd understand your situation. Practical things mattered too: cost, availability and how easy it is to book.

Research sets the rules

Studies on matching say to put safety first and show a short list of two or three. They also say to give little weight to the specialties therapists list for themselves, because therapists aren't good judges of their own strengths.

How it works

  1. 1

    Safety comes first

    Before any questions, a screen points to Lifeline and 000 for anyone who needs help today. Matching never gets in the way of urgent support.

  2. 2

    A few questions about what matters

    Up to 15 short questions about practical needs, what you'd like from a therapist, and any shared background that matters to you. Nothing about diagnoses or symptoms. Some answers can be marked as must-haves.

  3. 3

    Must-haves first, then fit

    Therapists who miss a must-have are set aside. Of the rest, those trained in an approach the research supports for what you'd like help with come first, and your preferences decide the order after that.

  4. 4

    A shortlist of three, with reasons

    Each therapist comes with where they line up with your answers and where they don't. If no one meets every must-have, Thera-Fits says so and shows the closest fits.

Demo only. The eight therapists are fictional, with free stock photos. Nothing you enter is saved or sent: your answers stay in your browser tab.
