# Therapist photos

Copies of `App/img/therapists/`, so the explainer doesn't depend on `App/`.

Free stock photos from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/), used for the 8 fictional therapist profiles. The people pictured are not therapists and have no connection to the profiles. The Pexels licence allows free use with no attribution required; credits are kept here so the source of each photo can be checked.

Cropped square around the face and resized to 256×256.

**Where they may appear in the explainer.** The Pexels licence says identifiable people "may not appear in a bad light". The explainer says negative things about some fictional profiles (for example "patients tended to get worse"), so photos appear only where nothing negative is said: the walkthrough's shortlist cards ("What Alex sees", `site/story.js`). Everywhere else, therapists are shown by their initials. Keep it that way.
